Chef Lo

I started Boomin Eats in the summer of 2020 with a new logo, a short menu and preorders through my DMs. Plates went out for pickup across the DMV. People came back for the honey cajun and the mac, then started asking me to cook their events.

Six years later I’m cooking in rooms I used to pray to be in. I still prep every plate like it’s going to a regular, because most of them are.

Comfort food is where I come from. Turning it into an experience is where I’m taking it.

  1. 2020

    First menu drops. Honey glazed salmon and shrimp, cilantro rice, Boomin Alfredo, all by preorder.

  2. 2021

    First catered events: a baby shower, a church outing and a birthday dinner.

  3. 2023

    Valentine’s giveaway. The winner got a private chef dinner for two.

  4. 2025

    Cooked a Women’s History Month dinner alongside Chef Tiffani Faison and wrote the night’s menu. Given 45 minutes to make a dish I’d never made, I plated half rack lamb with a fresco glaze and mint pea purée.

  5. 2026

    Worked the South Beach Wine & Food Festival. Chef spotlight at Georgetown University for Women’s History Month. In October, the first Boomin Eats food truck day in Temple Hills.
